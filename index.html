The neighborhood

Living in Woodland Hills

Woodland Hills is a residential neighborhood of the City of Los Angeles in the southwest San Fernando Valley. Its southern edge rises into the Santa Monica Mountains, where tree-lined hillside streets like Flamingo Street sit above the valley floor.

Everyday conveniences are close by: restaurants and shops along Ventura Boulevard, the Warner Center business district, and shopping and dining at Westfield Topanga and The Village. The US-101 Ventura Freeway connects Woodland Hills to the rest of Los Angeles and to Calabasas and the Conejo Valley, and Topanga State Park and other Santa Monica Mountains open space are nearby for hiking.
